\subsection{Adaptive Sampling}
\label{sec:adaptive}

Since evaluating $p_\theta(\cdot \mid x)$ is orders of magnitude cheaper
than executing a rollout, candidate initial states can be screened
before committing to the trajectory budget. The classifier
(Section~\ref{sec:classification}) provides a natural acquisition
signal: states receiving $\hat{y}(x) = \sep$ are precisely those where
additional data is most informative, enabling targeted sampling that
concentrates rollouts near the separatrix rather than spreading them
uniformly across~$\mathcal{X}$.

\textbf{Acquisition strategy: $\textsc{Direct}(r_{\mathrm{sep}})$.}
Each acquisition round collects $N$ new trajectories, governed by a
\emph{separatrix sampling ratio} $r_{\mathrm{sep}} \in [0,1]$.
A fraction $(1 - r_{\mathrm{sep}})$ of the budget provides non-adaptive
exploration: $N_{\mathrm{Unif}} = \lfloor (1 - r_{\mathrm{sep}}) N
\rfloor$ initial states are drawn uniformly from~$\mathcal{X}$ and
rolled out to obtain ground-truth labels. The remaining
$N_{\mathrm{Sep}} = N - N_{\mathrm{Unif}}$ trajectories target the
separatrix region via rejection sampling: candidates are drawn
uniformly from~$\mathcal{X}$, screened by computing $\hat{y}(x)$
via~\eqref{eq:threshold_rule}, and retained only if
$\hat{y}(x) = \sep$; drawing and screening continue until
$N_{\mathrm{Sep}}$ candidates are accepted and rolled out. Setting
$r_{\mathrm{sep}} = 0$ recovers purely uniform sampling (the
non-adaptive baseline), while $r_{\mathrm{sep}} = 1$ devotes the entire
budget to the separatrix region. Alternative acquisition strategies
that leverage the nonconformity score (Section~\ref{sec:conformal})
are compared in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.

\textbf{Iterative procedure.}
The pipeline applies $\textsc{Direct}(r_{\mathrm{sep}})$ over $E$
iterations (Algorithm~\ref{alg:pipeline}). At each iteration the
generative model is retrained on the accumulated dataset, thresholds
$(\lambda^*, \delta^*)$ are re-optimized via~\eqref{eq:threshold_opt},
and $N$ new trajectories are acquired via
$\textsc{Direct}(r_{\mathrm{sep}})$ and appended to the training set.
A final round of retraining and threshold optimization is performed
after the last iteration.

\begin{algorithm}[h]
\caption{Adaptive \roa\ Estimation (Training)}
\label{alg:pipeline}
\footnotesize
\SetAlgoLined
\KwIn{Seed trajectories $\mathcal{D}_0$,
  pool of candidate initial states $\mathcal{P}$,
  rollout budget $N$ per iteration, ratio $r_{\mathrm{sep}}$,
  iterations $E$}
\KwOut{Trained model $p_\theta$, optimized thresholds
  $(\lambda^*, \delta^*)$}

$\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}} \leftarrow
  \text{pairs from } \mathcal{D}_0$\;

\For{$e = 1$ \KwTo $E$}{
    Train $p_\theta$ on $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}$\;
    Optimize $(\lambda^*, \delta^*)$
      via~\eqref{eq:threshold_opt} on validation split\;
    Acquire $N$ trajectories via
      $\textsc{Direct}(r_{\mathrm{sep}})$ from $\mathcal{P}$\;
    Add new pairs to $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}$\;
}
Retrain $p_\theta$ and re-optimize
  $(\lambda^*, \delta^*)$ on final
  $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{train}}$\;
\end{algorithm}
