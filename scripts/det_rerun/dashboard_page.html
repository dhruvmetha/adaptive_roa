Nominal threshold fit (λ*, δ*)

Every cell's own scoring: each epoch fits λ* and δ* on the threshold-fit pairs, and that λ/δ rule labels each test state success, failure, outcome-uncertain or invalid.

Metrics against training trajectories

One line per model and regime: color is the model, dash pattern the regime, and a hollow end point a cell still running. The x axis is thousands of training trajectories. Invalid % and outcome-uncertain % share one scale. "FM uniform, always" keeps FM's uniform line in view whatever the model and acquisition chips say. KL is the log score against the true outcome; FPR and FNR are over committed states.

Drag sideways on a chart to zoom all seven into a range of trajectories, drag up or down to zoom that chart’s values, or drag a box to do both. Double-click to zoom back out.

FPR-capped threshold fit (α_s = 1%)

The new cells: each epoch fits τ_s, τ_f and τ_inv on the val split, from the first 20 of its 100 MC draws, to maximize accuracy with false success claims at most 1% of true failures and false failure claims at most 5% of true successes (τ_s ≥ 0.5). Direct and ranked acquisition and the test labels use that rule; uniform and entropy read no thresholds, so they have no cells here. Quadrotor 3D at 110k (LQR) and 100k (PPO) are the 200k runs' first 11 epochs.

No FPR-capped cell in this setting has banked an epoch yet.
